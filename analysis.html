🎬 Prepare full gameplay for ChatGPT

Select a recording and generate timestamped contact sheets. Your video is processed locally in Safari; no video is uploaded to our website.

1. Select video

For your own YouTube streams, download the recording from YouTube Studio first. Public YouTube links alone cannot be processed here.

2. Choose coverage

24 frames

For a one-hour video, 24 frames means approximately one frame every 2½ minutes. This is a sampling overview, not continuous full-video AI analysis. Repeat with narrower time windows for important plays.

Choose a video to begin.

3. Send to ChatGPT

Use Share image to send the timestamped sheet into ChatGPT, or save it to Photos/Files and attach it to our conversation.

The sheet is only a sparse sample of your video. A screenshot cannot prove exact button presses, meter timing, or controller inputs.
